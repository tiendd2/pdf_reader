Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Thank you for using PDF Scanner & Reader. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

PDF Scanner & Reader does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers.

All scanned documents, imported images, and PDF files remain stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them.

2. Camera Permission

The app requests access to your device's camera solely for scanning documents. Images captured by the camera are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

3. Photos and Files Permission

The app requests permission to access photos and files only to allow you to:

  • Import images or PDF files.
  • Save scanned documents.
  • Export or share files you choose.

The app only accesses files that you explicitly select.

4. Advertising

This application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Diagnostics information
  • App usage information

This information is collected directly by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob:
https://support.google.com/admob

5. Data Storage

All scanned documents and imported files are stored only on your device. The application does not provide cloud storage or maintain remote servers for user documents.

6. Data Security

Since all files remain on your device, you are responsible for protecting your device and the information stored on it.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
