# Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** August 4, 2026 ## PDF Scanner & Reader Privacy Policy Thank you for using **PDF Scanner & Reader**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information. ### 1. Information We Collect The application **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information** to our servers. All scanned documents and imported files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them using your device's built-in sharing features. ### 2. Camera Permission The app requests access to your device's camera solely to allow you to scan documents. Images captured by the camera are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server. ### 3. Photos and Files Permission The app may request permission to access photos, media, or files on your device so that you can: * Import images or PDF files. * Save scanned documents. * Export or share files that you choose. The app only accesses files that you explicitly select. ### 4. Advertising This application uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain information automatically, such as: * Device identifiers * Advertising ID * IP address * Diagnostic information * App usage information This information is collected directly by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob ### 5. Data Storage All documents created by the app remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. We do not maintain cloud storage or remote servers for your documents. ### 6. Children's Privacy The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ### 8.
